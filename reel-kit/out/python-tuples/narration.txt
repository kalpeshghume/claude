Python tuples, explained in six quick steps.

A tuple is a fixed group of values, written in round brackets.

Read items by position. Negative numbers count from the end.

A slice gives you a new, smaller tuple.

The count method tells how many times a value appears.

Unpacking splits a tuple into separate variables.

Tuples are immutable. You cannot change them after they are created.
