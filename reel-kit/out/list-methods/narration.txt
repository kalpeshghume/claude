Eleven Python list methods, explained with code. Let's go one by one.

The append method adds one item at the end of the list.

The clear method removes every item, and leaves an empty list.

The copy method makes a brand new list with the same items. Notice, a is b prints False.

The count method tells you how many times a value appears. Here, two shows up three times.

The extend method adds every item from another list, at the end.

The index method returns the position of the first match. Plum is at index two.

The insert method puts an item at any position you choose. Fig goes in at index one.

The pop method removes the item at a position, and gives it back to you.

The remove method deletes the first item that has that value.

The reverse method flips the order of the list, in place.

The sort method orders the list from smallest to largest.
